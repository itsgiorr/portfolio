About Me

Hello! I'm Jorge Urdaneta, a Venezuelan-born mechanical engineer at UT driven by curiosity, challenge, and impact. Moving to the U.S. at 15, I've navigated cultures, led student organizations, managed projects, and I'm building an autonomous flying ATV. My mindset is to always grow and give back.

My passion is robotics: designing autonomous systems that make life easier, safer, and better for people. Whether in the form of a car that keeps you safe or a humanoid that assists you — I want to build it and make an impact.

I pursue every project with purpose, grateful for the opportunities the world has given me. My goal is to repay that generosity through meaningful innovation that lasts generations.

Outside of academics and robotics, I love soccer, hiking, traveling, trying new foods, fashion, and painting shoes. I’m always exploring new ways to blend creativity and engineering.

I truly believe personality can take you a long way.
